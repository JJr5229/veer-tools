## FAQ

**Is it in colour?**
No. A lithophane shows your photo in light and shade, a little like a soft black-and-white print lit from behind. Unlit, it looks like a plain white panel.

**What if my photo isn't great?**
Send it anyway. We'll crop and adjust brightness, and the preview shows you what the finished piece will look like before anything is printed.

**Can I add a name and date?**
Yes, choose "Photo + text line" and type it in the personalization box exactly as it should read.

**How do I send my photo?**
After checkout, open your order in Etsy, tap "Message seller" and attach it. Please send the original file, not a screenshot.

**Can I have two photos?**
One photo per box. For two pets or two people side by side, choose Wide and send both; we'll place them together and show you the preview.

**Does it need batteries?**
No, it plugs into any USB charger or port. [Optional: battery puck light on request.]

**Do you keep my photo?**
We use it only to make your order and delete it once the order is complete. [Owner: confirm this matches what you do.]

## Owner workflow

1. When the buyer's photo arrives in Etsy Messages, save it to the order folder.
2. Open **Light Box Studio** (Pro), switch to **Photo lithophane**, pick the preset (Photo 6 × 4 · 162 × 112, Portrait 112 × 162, Square 120 × 120 or Wide 220 × 90), drop in the photo.
3. Adjust zoom, brightness and contrast (thinnest/thickest 0.8 / 3 mm is a good start). Add the text line if ordered.
4. Export the **proof PNG** and send it through Etsy Messages. Wait for approval.
5. Export the STL set. Print the front in white at fine layers, face-down; frames and backs in the chosen colour (batch them).
6. Fit the LED strip around the inside walls, cable out through the back hole, press the back in. Plug in and check the image before packing.
7. Delete the photo after the order is complete (if that's your stated policy).
