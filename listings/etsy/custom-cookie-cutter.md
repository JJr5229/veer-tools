## FAQ

**Can you really make a cutter from my kid's drawing?**
Yes. Send a clear photo. We trace the outline and send a preview so you can check it before we print.

**What's the stamp for?**
The cutter only cuts the outline. The stamp presses the inside details (faces, letters, logo lines) into the dough. It's mirrored so the result reads correctly.

**Is it dishwasher safe?**
No. Hand wash in warm, not hot, water. Dishwasher heat can warp PLA.

**Is it food safe?**
[Owner: answer only with what your filament's datasheet supports.] It's printed in [food-contact-grade] PLA. Like all 3D-printed cutters, it has fine layer lines, so wash it right after use and dry it fully. It's meant for short contact with dough, not storing food.

**My drawing has very thin lines.**
We thicken or simplify very thin parts so they cut cleanly. Fine details usually work better on the stamp than the cutter.

**Can I use it for clay or play-dough?**
Yes. Keep a clay cutter for clay only.

**Can you do a set of different shapes?**
Yes, place one order per drawing, or message us to arrange a set.

## Owner workflow

1. Save the drawing or logo from Etsy Messages.
2. Open **Vectorizer** and drop in the image. Turn on background removal; tidy until the trace is clean. Choose which colours count as the design ("Colours to use").
3. Set the output width to the ordered size (76 / 102 / 127 mm). Switch to **Print it in 3D**:
   - **Cookie cutter**: follows the outside outline, mirrored for flange-up use.
   - **Stamp** (if ordered): base follows the outline, design raised, mirrored so the impression reads correctly.
4. Screenshot the free STL preview as the outline proof and send it through Etsy Messages.
5. After approval, download the STLs (Pro), slice and print (flange on the bed for the cutter).
6. Check the edge for strings, test-cut a piece of dough or clay if you can, pack and ship.
