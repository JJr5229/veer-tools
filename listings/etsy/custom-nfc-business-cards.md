## Suggested price and cost note

**$39 / $64 / $129** for 5 / 10 / 25 (two-colour); +$6 / +$8 / +$16 for up to four colours.

Rough cost per card: ~11 g PLA (~$0.22), NTAG215 sticker ~$0.30–0.80, plus multi-colour purge waste. Purge is charged per colour change per layer, not per card, so a full plate of 10 cards costs about the same purge as one card. Batched, that lands around **$0.50–1.00 per card**. Add packaging (~$1–2) and Etsy fees (~10%). The real cost is the proof round, the print pause to drop in tags, and programming/testing each card.

## FAQ

**Do people need an app to tap my card?**
No. Modern iPhones and Android phones read NFC out of the box. They see a notification with your link and tap it.

**What can the card open?**
Any web link: your website, booking page, review page, portfolio, digital business card profile or social page. A web link works the same on iPhone and Android, so that's what we recommend.

**Can I change the link later?**
Yes, unless you ask us to lock it. Use a free NFC app (e.g. NFC Tools) to rewrite it. Or point the card at a page you control and update that page instead.

**Is the chip visible?**
No. It's sealed inside the card during printing.

**Can each card in a pack have a different link?**
Message us before ordering with the number of links and we'll confirm.

**Will it work through a phone case?**
Through most plastic and silicone cases, yes. Metal cases and card wallets with RFID blocking will stop it.

**What if a card doesn't scan?**
We test every card before shipping. If one doesn't work when it arrives, message us and we'll replace it.

## Owner workflow

1. Save the buyer's logo from Etsy Messages to the order folder. Copy their link and front text from the personalization field.
2. Open **NFC Studio** (Commercial license). Shape: rounded rectangle, 85.6 × 54 × 2.5 mm. Drop in the logo, set **Logo colours** (up to 3 + card body = 4), lock the palette to your filament colours.
3. Add company info (Pro): up to 3 lines, keep cap height at 3.5 mm or more. Tag: NTAG215, set **Tag thickness** to your tag's measured thickness, keep **Seal the tag inside the card** ticked.
4. Screenshot the front preview (it shows the finished card) and send it as the proof through Etsy Messages.
5. After approval, export the STL set. In Bambu Studio, drag all STLs in together → "single object with multiple parts" → Yes, assign filaments. Don't rotate. 0.2 mm layers, 100% infill, textured PEI plate. Fill the plate with the whole pack.
6. In Preview, add a pause at the Z height the tool reports. At the pause, drop a tag into each cavity, press flat, resume.
7. Program each card with NFC Tools (Write → URL), lock only if the buyer asked. Tap-test every card with an iPhone and an Android phone.
8. Pack, ship, mark shipped with tracking.
