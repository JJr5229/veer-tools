## Description

```
A simple light box for a name or a word: a frame, a thin white front with the letters inlaid in colour, and a back panel that presses in. Stick a warm-white USB LED strip around the inside, plug it in, and the front glows with the name in crisp silhouette.

WHAT'S IN THE FILES
- Frame: 180 × 80 × 40 mm, 2.4 mm walls, 5 mm corner radius
- Front: 1.2 mm white diffuser with the name inlaid 0.6 mm deep (one STL per colour)
- Back panel: locating rim that presses into the frame (0.3 mm standard fit), a 13 × 8 mm slot for the USB cable and two keyhole slots for wall hanging
- [Example fronts: "Olivia" in Pacifico, "Home" in Playfair, and a blank front — confirm which you include]
- A Bambu Studio print profile for each plate

HOW IT LIGHTS
Black letters block the light and read as a sharp silhouette. Coloured letters glow tinted. White PLA for the front is what makes it glow, so use white there even if the frame is another colour. The 40 mm depth keeps the LEDs far enough back to avoid hot spots.

PRINT NOTES
- Front: print face-down (already oriented). Colours via AMS; letters are flush with the face.
- Frame and back: any opaque PLA. Dark colours stop light leaking through the walls.
- No supports needed.
- If the back panel is too tight, sand the rim lightly. Snug and loose versions can be made in Light Box Studio (see below).

ASSEMBLY
1. Stick the LED strip around the inside walls of the frame, LEDs facing inward.
2. Feed the USB plug out through the slot in the back panel.
3. Press the back panel into the frame until the rim seats.
4. Plug into any USB charger. Hang it from the keyhole slots or stand it on a shelf.

YOUR OWN NAME, A LOGO OR A PHOTO
These files were made with Light Box Studio, a browser tool from Veer. The free version does names in 9 fonts at any size. Pro adds logos, photo lithophanes and batch names. Search "Veer Light Box Studio" to find it.
```
