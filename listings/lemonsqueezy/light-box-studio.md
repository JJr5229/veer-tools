## Long description

```markdown
Light Box Studio makes the files for a 3D-printed light box from a name, a logo or any photo. You get a frame, a white light-diffusing front and a back panel with a cable hole and keyhole slots, ready for a warm-white USB LED strip.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

## What it does

- **Names in 9 fonts:** Pacifico, Great Vibes, Dancing Script, Lobster, Baloo, Fredoka, Bebas Neue, Montserrat and Playfair, with an optional second line.
- **Logos and artwork, inlaid.** Text and artwork are set flush into a thin white front, one STL per colour for an AMS. White glows, colours glow tinted, black blocks the light, so black lettering reads as a crisp silhouette.
- **Photo lithophanes.** Any photo becomes a variable-thickness front: thin where it's bright, thick where it's dark. Zoom, brightness and contrast controls, and optional text over the photo.
- **The box.** Presets for a name sign (180 × 80 mm), square (120 × 120), wide (220 × 90) and a 6 × 4 in photo (162 × 112, landscape or portrait), or any custom size. Depth (40 mm default), wall, corner radius, front thickness and colour depth are all adjustable.
- **The back panel** has a locating rim that presses into the frame (snug, standard or loose fit), a hole for the USB cable and keyhole slots for hanging.
- **Lit preview** shows the box glowing before you print.
- **Proof PNG:** a lit mock-up to send your customer before you print.
- **Batch names:** paste one name per line, straight from your Etsy orders. You get one shared frame and back, a front for every name, and a proof PNG per name.
- **Watertight meshes** on every part, tested on text, multi-colour logos, lithophanes and batch builds.

## Who it's for

Makers selling personalised night lights, logo signs and photo gifts, and anyone who wants to make one for a nursery, a shop counter or a memorial.

## What you'll need to build one

A 3D printer (a multi-colour printer for inlaid logos), white PLA for the front, and a 5 V USB LED strip (warm white, about 50 cm) stuck around the inside walls. A battery puck light on the back panel works too. At least 30 mm of depth avoids hot spots.
```
