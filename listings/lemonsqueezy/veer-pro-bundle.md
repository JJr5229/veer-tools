## Long description

```markdown
Veer Pro unlocks every Veer browser tool with one license key, and every tool we add later.

## What's included

- **NFC Studio:** logo to multi-colour STL files for NFC business cards and keychains, with the tag sealed inside. Up to 4 colours, company text, clean back.
- **Vectorizer Pro:** sized SVG with colour layers, one SVG per colour, DXF for laser cutters, batch convert, and STL cookie cutters, stamps, stencils and multi-colour plaques.
- **Light Box Studio:** names, logos and photo lithophanes as 3D-printable light boxes, with lit previews, proof PNGs and batch names.
- **Future Veer tools**, as they're released.

[Option A — bundle includes selling rights:] Includes the Commercial license for NFC Studio and Light Box Studio, so you can sell what you print.
[Option B — Pro rights only:] Includes Pro for every tool. To sell prints from NFC Studio or Light Box Studio, add the Commercial upgrade.

## Who it's for

Makers who use more than one of the tools, or sell several kinds of printed goods: cards, signs, light boxes, cutters.
```
