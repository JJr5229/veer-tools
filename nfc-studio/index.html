Match these to your slicer profile exactly. The tag cavity's floor and ceiling are snapped onto real layer boundaries — if they land mid-layer the slicer has to print a layer that's half solid and half bridging over the void, which shows up as ridges on the back of the card.

7 · Print notes
Orientation: the model is already built face-down — the logo sits on the build plate as the first layers, and the NFC pocket opens upward. Don't rotate it.

Plate: a smooth PEI or textured plate becomes the card's front finish. Textured hides layer lines best.

Settings: 0.2 mm layers, 100% infill (the card is thin — solid is faster than sparse + top layers), 0 elephant-foot if you can.

Colours: in Bambu Studio, right-click the object → Change filament per part, or click each part in the object list and set its filament. Colour changes only happen in the first few layers, so purge waste stays small.

Tag: drop the NFC sticker into the pocket after printing and cover with a printed lid, vinyl sticker, or a smear of glue. Keep the card away from metal surfaces — metal detunes the antenna and kills the read.
