How it works

Four panels, top to bottom

Open the tool and work down the left side. The preview updates as you type.

1Front

Pick Name & graphics (text and artwork set flush into a thin white front) or Photo lithophane. Type up to two lines in 9 fonts, from Pacifico and Great Vibes to Bebas Neue and Playfair. Add a logo or photo and choose 1 to 3 image colours.

2Box

Choose a size preset or type your own. Set depth, wall, corner radius, front thickness, and the frame and front colours.

3Back panel & light

Turn on the cable hole and keyhole slots, and pick how tightly the back fits: snug, standard or loose.

4Batch names

Paste a list of names to make one front and one proof per name, sharing a single frame and back.

What you print

A frame, a front and a back

Two prints. The frame and front load as one multi-part object; the back panel prints on its own.

Frame1_box/1_frame.stl

The box walls, in any opaque filament. It prints front-down together with the front, so the face comes off a smooth plate clean.2.4 mm walls · 40 mm deep · 5 mm corners

Front1_box/2_front.stl + colours

The diffuser, in white PLA. For names and logos, each colour is its own STL set flush into the face, ready for an AMS. For photos, one lithophane of varying thickness.1.2 mm face · 0.6 mm colour depth · lithophane 0.8–3 mm

Back panel2_back/back.stl

A flat plate with a locating rim that presses into the frame. It has a slot for a USB cable and two keyhole slots for hanging it on a wall.2.4 mm plate · 4 mm rim · fit 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.45 mm

Lighting

What goes inside

The box is built around a cheap USB LED strip. Here is a cross-section of the name sign.

  • The light

    A 5 V USB LED strip, warm white, about 50 cm long, stuck around the inside walls. It runs from a USB phone charger. A battery puck light stuck to the back panel works too.

  • Depth

    Keep the box at least 30 mm deep. Closer than that and you see the individual LEDs as hot spots on the front. The presets start at 40 mm.

  • The cable

    Run it out through the hole in the back panel before you press the panel in. The tool can leave the hole out if you use a puck light.

  • Hanging

    Two keyhole slots in the back panel, with an 8 mm opening for the screw head, hang it flat on a wall. Or just stand it on a shelf.

FAQ

Before your first print

Which filament should I use for the front?

White PLA. It is the diffuser, so it should let light through evenly. For names and logos, black text blocks light and reads as a crisp silhouette; any other colour glows tinted. The frame and back can be any opaque colour. An AMS holds 4 filaments, and the tool warns you if a design needs more.

What layer height for a lithophane?

0.12–0.16 mm layers in white PLA at 100% infill look best. Name and logo fronts are fine at 0.2 mm. The README in every download repeats the settings.

How does the back attach?

The back panel has a 4 mm locating rim that presses into the back of the frame. No screws or glue. Pick snug (0.2 mm), standard (0.3 mm) or loose (0.45 mm) to suit your printer. If it comes out too tight or too loose, change the fit and re-export.

Can I sell the light boxes I print?

Yes, with Commercial ($79, one-time). Free and Pro are for personal use and gifts.

Do my customers need to use the tool?

No. You make the file and send them the proof image to approve. They never see Light Box Studio. In batch mode every name gets its own proof.png, ready to attach to an Etsy message.

Which printer and slicer does it work with?

The files are standard STL. The README gives the steps for Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer: drag every file in 1_box onto the plate together and load them as one object with multiple parts. Name and logo fronts are multi-colour, so they need a multi-material setup such as a Bambu AMS.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Light Box Studio runs entirely in your browser. Customer photos and names stay on your computer.
