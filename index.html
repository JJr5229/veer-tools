How it works

From artwork to the build plate in three steps.

  1. Drop in your artwork

    A PNG, JPG, SVG or WebP logo, a photo, or just a name typed in. The tool separates the colours and traces real vector outlines, not pixel stairs.

  2. Tune it in the live preview

    Pick colours per AMS slot, set the size in millimetres, move the art, add text. The 2D and 3D previews update as you go.

  3. Download and print

    You get one watertight STL per filament. Drag them all into Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer, load them as one object with multiple parts, and pick a filament for each.

FAQ

Questions makers ask.

Does it work with my printer and slicer?

The files are standard STL in millimetres, so any slicer opens them. The multi-colour tools give you one STL per filament. In Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer, select them all, drag them onto the plate together and answer yes to “load as a single object with multiple parts”, then pick a filament for each part. The tools are made with Bambu printers and the AMS in mind, but any multi-material setup works the same way.

What's the difference between Pro and Commercial?

The features are the same. Pro is for your own prints: your business cards, gifts, things for friends. Commercial adds the right to sell what you print, for example light boxes or NFC cards on Etsy or at markets.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro and Commercial are one-time purchases per tool. You get a license key, enter it once in the tool, and it stays unlocked.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All the work happens in your browser, so once a tool has loaded it doesn't need the internet. A Pro unlock is stored in your browser and keeps working offline; it re-checks the key now and then when you're back online.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Open a tool and start.

Which browser should I use?

A recent Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop works best. The 3D preview uses WebGL, which every current desktop browser supports.

Can I get a refund?

Contact us within 14 days if a tool doesn't work for you. You can reach us on Instagram at @getveer or through veersolutions.com.
